About

Beauty care in Nnewi, and across Anambra.

TRICIA-V Unisex Salon and Spa is at 40 Bishop Uzodike Rd, Nnewi, led by Vivian Udechukwu. Pedicure, manicure and nail art, facials, Moroccan radiance therapy, waxing, body treatments, hair and barbing, semi-permanent tattoos and skin consultations, all in one place.

Cannot come to us? Most of our treatments can be done at your home, anywhere in Anambra. Message us on WhatsApp, tell us what you need, and we will arrange a time.

  • Premium products in every treatment.
  • Home service across Anambra - we come to you.
  • WhatsApp replies during opening hours.
